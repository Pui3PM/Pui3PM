Precision boundary

Head and anchor guidance use validated body/face-adjacent pose landmarks. Clicker blade, arrow point, bow cant and stabilizer reaction are not reported unless a future dedicated equipment tracker reaches sufficient confidence. V5 Dev4-X1 does not invent unsupported values. Release classification uses validated shot sequence, hand/elbow trajectory and coarse pose-hand evidence when visible; fine finger timing, clicker blade and arrow-point measurements stay unavailable until dedicated tracking has sufficient evidence.

Skeleton overlay & captured evidence

The live skeleton is a display/diagnostic layer only. Shot key frames are saved from the raw camera image without the skeleton burned in, so the coach can inspect the original picture. Pose landmarks and measurements are analyzed separately; turning the visible overlay off does not disable analysis while Pose remains enabled.
